title: Welcome to the Forgotten Skies
---
A maintenance woman enters a building, checks in with security and moves through the lobby. She scans her badge to enter the elevator, "Welcome Bradley Allencort," reads the screen. About a month prior, Bradley took a photo of the bar he visits regularly and posted it on his Facebook with the caption, "I live for Friday night karaoke here!!" The following Friday a woman accidentally bumps into him at the bar and spills his drink on her. After apologizing profusely he buys her another. During the conversation, however, a small device was hanging from her belt loop near his badge. She successfully cloned the badge of a C-Suite assistant and walked away as Bradley Allencort and is now using this digital badge to freely attack this building unquestioned.

Welcome to the Forgotten Skies.

As a bartender turned Information Security and Risk Management (ISRM), I approach this blog with stories, personality, and sarcasm. This is a place for me to publish my thoughts, ideas, and journey through the infosec space. It's also a place for me to put work instructions I create for fiddly processes I will inevitably forget.
