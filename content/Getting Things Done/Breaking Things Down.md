When you can't start on a project, it's often because the scope is too big, an pattern that is deviously easy to fall into. When this happens, look at all the steps you've outlined to get to the final goal you've laid out for yourself. Take the time to write these down.

After doing so, choose one of the steps to analyze. Can this step be broken down further? If no, move to a different step. It's also important to note that just because the answer right now is "no," doesn't mean it will stay this way. Sometimes the answer is "no" simply because you don't currently have the skillset to do so. Take a second to note this down before moving on. Also take note of why you feel this is.

Okay, you've found a step that can be broken down. Great! What now? This step now becomes the goal and gets broken down into even more steps. Now, go do that first step, then the second, and so on until this new goal is reached. Do that until all the goals are reached. 

Whenever you come across a goal that you can't achieve, it's likely because the step is larger than you thought it was going to be when you came up with that step.
