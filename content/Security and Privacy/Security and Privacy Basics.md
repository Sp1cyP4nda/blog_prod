The lifestyle
	I dislike when people arrogantly express "mmmm \<blank\> is a lifestyle" while looking down their nose at you. But, security and privacy are, in fact, lifestyle changes. These changes are not for everyone, and that's okay. Hell, there are some practices that I do not ascribe to. What's important here is to know your options and know the pros and cons of each so you can decide for yourself which you will adopt, which you will discard, and which you want to build towards.
